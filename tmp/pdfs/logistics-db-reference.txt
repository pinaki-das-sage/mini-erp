4. ME_LOG_RESOURCE_ASSIGNMENTS
Active assignment uniqueness
Only one active assignment is allowed per trip, vehicle, and driver. Ordinary unique
constraints cannot be used because released history must remain, so function-based unique
indexes are required.

Active order claim uniqueness
The requirement says a Sales Order may have at most one active claim. The current index
enforces that only for 
STATE = 'A'
. It does not prevent a Sales Order with a 
D
 claim from
being claimed by another Trip. MiniERP Logistics Business Requirements v1.9 does not
explicitly settle whether dispatched historical claims must continue blocking future claims, so
this remains an open design decision rather than an unsupported schema change.

Business Rules Enforced Outside DDL
The following requirements cannot be completely represented using ordinary
PK/FK/UK/CHECK constraints and must be validated by the Logistics domain services inside
the same Oracle transaction. Do not convert these cross-table or cross-module rules into
artificial CHECK constraints.
1. 
Driver profile is ACTIVE.
2. 
Linked User is ACTIVE and has the Driver role.
3. 
Driver licence covers planned departure and is revalidated at dispatch.
4. 
Vehicle and Driver availability is rechecked under database locks.
5. 
Sales Order is fully allocated before it can be claimed.
6. 
SalesLine, Product, Reservation, Company and Quantity belong to the same valid business
lineage.
7. 
The entire Sales Order is claimed. Individual Sales lines cannot be independently selected.
8. 
Exact Trip planning load is: 
SUM(TripLine.QUANTITY *
TripLine.UNIT_WEIGHT_KG)
9. 
Planned load must not exceed selected 
Vehicle.CAPACITY_KG
. Exact capacity is
permitted.
10. 
Dispatch must include every ACTIVE TripOrderClaim and every corresponding TripLine
atomically.
11. 
At dispatch: 
ME_LOG_TRIPS.DISPATCH_LOAD_KG = SUM(frozen
ME_LOG_TRIP_LINES.LINE_LOAD_KG)
12. 
Dispatch must assign stable 
MANIFEST_ID
 / 
MANIFEST_REFERENCE
 and 
SHIPMENT_LINE_ID
 values exactly once.
13. 
Delivery must enforce: 
ME_LOG_DELIVERY_OUTCOMES.DELIVERED_QUANTITY =
ME_LOG_TRIP_LINES.SHIPPED_QUANTITY
14. 
Partial, excess, fractional and duplicate delivery must fail.
15. 
Delivery may only be performed by the currently assigned eligible Driver.
16. 
Close is allowed only when every shipment line has a successful DeliveryOutcome and
Sales in-transit quantity is zero.
17. 
ME_AUDIT event and replay/idempotency result must commit in the same transaction as
the business action.
18. 
Any failed posting must roll back Logistics, Sales, Inventory, audit and replay effects
together.

Key design principle:
 Derived attributes are not persisted where their current value can be
deterministically obtained from existing authoritative records. Availability and active-trip
information are derived from the active ResourceAssignment; Trip load and capacity
presentation is derived from planning data before dispatch and frozen snapshot data after
dispatch. Dispatch document attributes are exposed through Manifest and ManifestLine
read-only projections rather than separate persistence tables. Values that become historical
business facts at dispatch, such as shipment quantity, line load, vehicle registration, and
destination, are persisted as immutable snapshots even when their original value was
calculated or copied from another source.
